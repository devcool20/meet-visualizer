The unflattering case. When the presenter is centred, the outer third is not clean wall — the card partially overlaps shoulder and near-arm, so the backdrop under the glass swings from lit skin to dark clothing inside a single card height. This is why the glass sits at 0.62 alpha rather than the original 0.45.
