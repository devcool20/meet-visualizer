Presenter HUD 13 / error

Error

Generation failed. The copy answers the two questions a presenter actually has, in order: did the room see anything? (“nothing was shown”) and what do I do? (“Try again”, plus the hotkey). It is a deep oxblood rather than a signal red — a failed card is a non-event, and mid-call is the worst possible moment to alarm someone.

Tone
Fill rgba(48,17,15,0.90) with a 34%-opacity #F0736A hairline. The dot is the only saturated red. Deliberately quieter than Meet’s own #EA4335.
Copy contract
Line 1 names the outcome (“No card this time”). Line 2 names the cause and the blast radius (“Engine timed out · nothing was shown”). Never a stack trace, never an error code.
Recovery
A real Try again button plus the hotkey, so it is reachable without a mouse mid-presentation.
Dismissal
Auto-dismisses after 6 s, or on the next hotkey press. It never blocks the next attempt.
Motion
Fades in over 150 ms with no travel, in both motion modes. An error should not spring.
