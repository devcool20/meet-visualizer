How cards get triggered

Pick one. This is the single biggest difference in how Stash Live feels day to day, so it’s worth thirty seconds of reading.

Hold‑to‑talk

Default

You hold Alt + Shift + Space and talk. When you let go, Stash Live writes a card from what you just said and shows it. Nothing appears unless you ask for it.

Good for

  • Client calls and demos where a surprise card would be awkward
  • Talking about numbers you didn’t prepare a card for

The catch

  • You have to remember the keys
  • A card takes a second or two to arrive

Ambient

Stash Live listens the whole meeting and shows one of your saved cards when it hears a phrase you set up — no keys, no thinking about it. It never writes anything new; it only picks from cards you already approved.

Good for

  • Repeating the same pitch several times a week
  • Presenting hands‑free while sharing your screen

The catch

  • It can fire when you didn’t mean it to
  • Your microphone is being transcribed for the whole call
