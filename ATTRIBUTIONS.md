### Wikipedia / Wikimedia

[Wikipedia](https://www.wikipedia.org/) content and [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/) images are used under the
[Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International License (CC BY-SA 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).

AI-generated cards that include Wikipedia-sourced content display a `Source: Wikipedia · <title>` footer. Cards without a verified source display `Unverified · AI-generated`.

Generated card images are proxied through the Stash Live engine's image cache and served with `Access-Control-Allow-Origin: *` to the browser's video pipeline. Original image URLs are never exposed to the client.

The Wikimedia REST API is queried with a descriptive `User-Agent` header (`StashLive/0.1`) as required by the [Wikimedia API usage guidelines](https://foundation.wikimedia.org/wiki/Policy:User-Agent_policy).
